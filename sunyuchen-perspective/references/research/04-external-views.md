## 1. SEC 2023 起诉书：具体指控（原文引语）

来源：[SEC Press Release 2023-59（2023-03-22）](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2023-59)【二手·监管官方原文】

三项法定指控（诉状提交 SDNY，No. 1:23-cv-02433，法官 Edgardo Ramos）：

- **未注册证券发行（Section 5）**：孙及三家全资公司（Tron Foundation Limited、BitTorrent Foundation Ltd.、Rainberry Inc.）通过多轮 "bounty programs" 和月度空投向包括美国投资者在内的公众发售 TRX 与 BTT。
- **洗售交易操纵市场（Section 17(a)(3)）**："From at least April 2018 through February 2019, Sun allegedly directed his employees to engage in more than **600,000 wash trades** of TRX between two crypto asset trading platform accounts he controlled, with between **4.5 million and 7.4 million TRX wash traded daily**." 孙本人在二级市场卖出 TRX，"generating proceeds of **$31 million** from illegal, unregistered offers and sales of the token."
- **付费名人推广不披露（Section 17(b)）**：同日起诉 8 位名人——Lindsay Lohan、Jake Paul、Soulja Boy、Austin Mahone、Kendra Lust、Lil Yachty、Ne-Yo、Akon。其中 6 人合计支付超 40 万美元和解（不承认不否认）。Lohan 收 1 万美元发推，Paul 收价值 2.5 万美元的加密资产。

Gary Gensler 定性原话："As alleged, Sun and his companies not only targeted U.S. investors in their unregistered offers and sales, generating millions in illegal proceeds at the expense of investors, but they also coordinated wash trading on an unregistered trading platform to create the misleading appearance of active trading in TRX."

执法部主任 Gurbir Grewal："Sun and others used an age-old playbook to mislead and harm investors... This is the very conduct that the federal securities laws were designed to protect against **regardless of the labels Sun and others used**."（"regardless of the labels" 直接回应孙方"utility token"抗辩）

孙本人 2023-03-22 回应【一手，推特，经 [crypto.news](https://crypto.news/justin-sun-and-crypto-twitter-react-to-secs-recent-crusade/) 与 [Popular Information](https://popular.info/p/a-chinese-national-charged-with-fraud) 转录】：
- "We believe the complaint lacks merit, and in the meantime will continue building the most decentralized financial system."
- "the SEC's regulatory framework for digital assets is still in its infancy and in need of further development"

表达DNA注记：被起诉当天的话术是"贬低对手框架成熟度 + 宣示继续建设"，不承认具体事实、不逐条反驳，把案件重新定义成"行业 vs 监管"的宏大叙事。

## 3. The Verge 2022 长篇调查：《The many escapes of Justin Sun》

来源：[The Verge，Christopher Harland-Dunaway，2022-03-09](https://www.theverge.com/c/22947663/the-many-escapes-of-justin-sun)【二手·调查报道】。方法论：15 名匿名信源（现任/前任员工，"fear retaliation"）+ 数百页泄露的内部 Tron 文件。孙拒绝回应提问；其诉讼律所 Harder LLP 称文章是 "yet another attempt by Mr. Harland-Dunaway to impair the reputation of Justin Sun"。

核心指控（均为前员工信源说法）：

- **ICO 抢跑禁令**：2017-09 中国 ICO 禁令前夜完成 TRX 售卖（募 7000 万美元），随后从首尔飞旧金山。"Sun pushed TRX to finish its token sale the day before the ban was announced. Sun had been tipped off by Changpeng 'CZ' Zhao.""They were in it together," a former employee told me.（CZ 后来说两人 "We have only talked business and haven't really 'hung out' in any way."——但 2019 年两人同游日内瓦湖。矛盾并列。）
- **北京"做市团队"**：BitTorrent CFO Dipak Joshi 从北京峰会回来后 "looking shaken"，告诉同事 "they were running an insider trading team in Beijing"。做市负责人 Baolong Xu 向员工解释其工作是 "to make sure the TRX price is at some level Justin wants"——利好消息公告前买入、公告后卖出。孙的北京副手被问"market making 是什么"时笑着说 "You know how the Chinese folks do it, right?" 并称 "You know those folks that have a lot of money and don't have a lot of brains? You can influence them to do whatever you want them to do."
- **律师购物（lawyer shopping）**：面试 SEC 前合规官 David Labhart 时，孙不想要合规体系，只想要他 "proving that TRX is not a security, but a utility token"。Labhart 犹豫，孙 "'asserted his dominance and was like, I'm the boss, and you'll be working under me, and these are my needs.'" Labhart 入职后因 BTT 空投等问题辞职。孙随后找香港律师写 "TRX is a utility" 意见书。一位律师评价："You're not looking for counsel — you're looking for a yes man."
- **Poloniex "Fake the KYC"**：孙在会上喊 "'Fake the KYC!'... 'Fake it!'"；自动 KYC 系统形同虚设，"Didn't matter if they submitted a pic of Daffy Duck." 公司架构迁至塞舌尔，"The corporate structure is like a Russian nesting doll of obfuscation."
- **Operation Couch Cushions（沙发缝行动）**：收集用户误存、无法找回的比特币"粉尘"约 230 BTC（时值超 1000 万美元），员工称孙反复追问 "**Where's my 300 Bitcoin?**"。UCLA 教授 Andrew Verstein："It's definitely a crime to convert customer funds for personal use."
- **逃生通道组合**：马耳他租公寓、以"Yuchen Sun"注册公司、为父母各投 5 万欧元申请身份；自称持有圣基茨和尼维斯护照；向员工吹嘘要买几内亚比绍护照；在美国控制至少 13 个银行账户，"He thought taxes were stupid." 一次关于法律风险的会议上，孙说："**What's the big deal? The worst thing may be that I will never come to the US anymore.**"
- **FBI/IRS/SDNY 大陪审团调查**（截至 2022 年 3 月进行中）：传票列出的潜在指控包括 wire fraud、money laundering、failure to register a security 等。
- 结尾点睛（前员工解释孙为何敢做）："**If he breaks so many laws at such a rapid pace, it'd be impossible for anyone to catch up to him.** So far, no one has. And so far, it's always worked."
- 另一个被转述的画面：北京峰会上孙自称 "Chairman Mao"，称 BitTorrent 高管为 "his generals"，要一起屠杀竞争对手；BitTorrent CEO 反问 "Weren't all of Mao's generals killed?" 孙答 "Yes, yes."

## 4. 白皮书抄袭指控（IPFS/Filecoin）

来源：[TRONOrigin 主网上线史（含原始出处链）](https://tronorigin.io/learn/mainnet-independence/)、BuzzFeed News、Digital Asset Research（DAR）。【二手】

- 2018-01-07，IPFS/Filecoin 论文作者 **Juan Benet** 亲自下场：TRON 白皮书 "**mostly copied from other projects, or is super basic p2p passed off as original. Zero references.**" 并把 PDF 存档到 IPFS，"in case they take it down."（首发于回复 Joystream 创始人 Bedeho Mender 的帖子）
- 2018-01-08，孙的回应【一手，X 帖子】：原始白皮书是中文版、有 "a very detailed reference"；英日韩西版本 "are translated by the volunteers. The translation missed numerous important details not just reference."——即"志愿者翻译漏了参考文献"抗辩。
- 2018-03，BuzzFeed News 用 Quetext 跑查重：**相似度超 15%**，含共享图表、段落与措辞。TRON 随后从官网撤下原版白皮书、换发新版。
- 2018-06，DAR 发现 java-tron 客户端代码 "copied verbatim or slightly modified from EthereumJ, still without appropriate reference"，违反 LGPL-3.0；回顾 Benet 的并排对比，**至少 9 页**抄自 Filecoin/IPFS 论文，判定抄袭规模 "unlikely to have been a mere mistranslation"——直接否定孙的翻译抗辩。
- Vitalik Buterin 2018-04-06 推特嘲讽（孙发"波场比以太坊好的 7 个理由"后，Vitalik 补第 8 条）："**8. Better white paper writing capability (Ctrl+C + Ctrl+V much higher efficiency than keyboard typing new content)**"（[ihodl 转录](https://ihodl.com/lifestyle/2018-07-22/who-justin-sun/)、[Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/vitalik-buterin-continues-troll-tron-184140413.html)）。【二手转一手帖】

表达DNA注记：孙从未公开承认抄袭，文件被悄悄替换而非更正（"the paper was quietly replaced rather than corrected"）。应对模式：给一个技术上无法完全证伪的解释（翻译遗漏），不恋战，直接换掉证据载体。

## 6. 社区绰号语义

- **孙割**："割"=割韭菜。"孙宇晨曾多次高价喊单后价格回落，被社区戏称为'孙割'"（[脚本之家币圈外号考据](https://www.jb51.net/blockchain/1038268jcwq.html)）。币圈顺口溜："**孙割一笑，生死难料**"（[币圈子](https://www.120btc.com/baike/qukuai/452829716.html)）。36kr 2022 年标题即"割完虚拟币，再割NFT，孙宇晨成就'孙割'"。语义：把一切公开动作先验地解读为出货前奏，他的笑容=危险信号。【二手】
- **孙哥**：半亲昵称呼，2019-11 在线发钱后大规模流行（见 5b），后成为币圈媒体中性称呼（链上分析师余烬等均直呼"孙哥"）。语义：对其"真发钱、真敢玩"的江湖式承认，褒贬参半。

## 7. 起诉 Bloomberg 亿万富翁榜（2025-08）

来源：[Protos 2025-08-14](https://protos.com/justin-sun-sues-bloomberg/)、[特拉华联邦法院意见书](https://www.ded.uscourts.gov/sites/ded/files/opinions/25-1007.pdf)、[CourtListener 案卷](https://www.courtlistener.com/docket/71089620/sun-v-bloomberg-lp/)。【二手+官方法律文书】

- 起因：孙为入选 Bloomberg Billionaires Index 主动向彭博提供钱包地址等资产证明，后在群聊中单方面宣布 "All information shared within the group is strictly confidential... Once the verification is complete, the data must be deleted." 彭博记者 Tom Maloney 回复："**Nobody at Bloomberg agreed to the terms sent by Justin, weeks after the data was shared with us.**" 且 "never agreed to simply do a top-line number."
- 2025-08-11 孙在特拉华联邦法院起诉（public disclosure of private facts + promissory estoppel），称公开持仓明细会让他面临 "significant and imminent risk" of theft/hacking/kidnapping（"wrench attacks"），并称持仓明细 "like a social security number, a piece of private, personal information in which the public has no legitimate interest." 同时主张 "the public has an interest in ensuring journalists keep their promises to their sources."
- 结果：彭博的亿万富豪档案在孙起诉**之前已发布**；2025-09-22，首席法官 Colm F. Connolly **驳回**孙的临时限制令/初步禁令申请（DENIED）。彭博抗辩理由：事先限制出版是对宪法第一修正案的侵犯，"available only in the rarest circumstances."
- 批评者视角（Citation Needed 标题即定性）："Justin Sun has sued Bloomberg for publishing details about his wealth that **he himself provided** to qualify for their Billionaire Index."（[citationneeded.news](https://www.citationneeded.news/justin-sun-bloomberg-lawsuit/)）
- 该案副产品：彭博档案写明 "Sun owns the cryptocurrency exchange HTX"，持股约 90%（基于其代表 2025 年 5 月提供的信息）——而孙此前多年公开否认拥有 HTX（只承认"顾问"）。Protos："It's not clear why Sun has previously denied this ownership."【矛盾并列：公开否认 vs 自己提供给彭博的核验材料】
- 表达DNA注记：又要榜单带来的合法性背书，又要单方控制披露粒度；先给数据、事后补保密条款、再用诉讼封锁——"先利用规则入场，再尝试改写规则"。
